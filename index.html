FAQ

How often do you upload?

I try to put out about two videos a month. Hopefully they're good!

What is Cilkocrate?

Cilkocrate is a series I made up. It has its own list of levels, top players and music, and the full list lives on the Cilkocrate website.

Where can I see the full level list?

Hit the gold Visit the Cilkocrate Website button, or use the link under the Top 20. Both open the full list.

How do I turn the music off?

Use the Music button in the bottom right corner of the page. It works in full screen too, and the arrow next to it has the volume.

Are the stats and progress bars live?

No, I update them by hand, so they might be a little behind. Check the date at the very bottom of the page.
